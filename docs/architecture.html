Agentic Research Loop

The realtime voice model decides when a request is too complex to answer in one tool call and hands off to run_research_agent. The orchestrator then runs its own loop on GPT-4.1 mini — pulling 10-Ks from EDGAR, prices from Finnhub, reflecting until the briefing is complete, and handing back a deterministic summary that the voice model reads verbatim instead of paraphrasing.

PLAN

03
REFLECT

04
SYNTHESIZE
